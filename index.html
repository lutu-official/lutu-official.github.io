Legal — what protects you

CPA Section 17(3)

Where an order is later placed, a supplier may require a reasonable deposit. No deposit or payment is collected at the waiting list stage — only if and when you choose to order.

ECTA Section 44

If you place an order and pay a deposit, you have a 7-day cooling-off period from that date to cancel for a full refund — no reason required. Joining this waiting list involves no payment.

ECTA Section 46

ECTA requires order fulfilment within 30 days unless otherwise agreed in writing. Delivery timing is agreed with you individually when your order is confirmed — not at the point of joining the waiting list.

POPIA

Your personal information is used only to process your order. It is not sold or shared with third parties. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Waiting List Terms and Conditions
