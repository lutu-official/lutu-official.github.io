How it works

1

You message us

Tell us the piece, the occasion, and the date you need it for.

2

We take your measurements and fabric choice

In person in Cape Town, or guided over WhatsApp if you're elsewhere. We confirm the fabric and the final price before anything is cut.

3

50% deposit, we begin

The deposit reserves your place in the month's commissions and pays for the cloth. Your pattern is cut to your measurements alone.

4

Your delivery window, balance on completion

Your production and delivery window is confirmed to you in writing before you pay your deposit. You see the piece before you settle the balance.
