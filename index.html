Questions

The things brands ask first.

How long does it take?

About three weeks from the shoot day for a standard delivery, or about ten days if you add rush. We confirm your window in writing before you pay a deposit, and we tell you straight away if anything puts it at risk.

What if my products get held at customs?

It happens, and it is the main reason we ask you not to ship before the quote is confirmed. Ship as temporary samples for a photo shoot. Duties and shipping in both directions are yours. If a shipment is delayed we move your shoot day rather than shoot without the products.

Can you do hijab styling?

Yes. You set the modesty level — hijab, non-hijab, no faces, or hands only — and we cast and style to it. A modest styling specialist can be arranged, and we can run a closed set on a private location.

Will the models be suitable for my brand?

You approve casting before the shoot day. Tell us the look you need and we send options. Nothing is shot with a model you have not agreed to.

Do you use AI, and how much?

Hero images are photographs of real models on real locations. AI is used to extend those into catalogue variations when you need volume — more angles, more colourways, more crops — and a person checks every single AI image before it reaches you. We will tell you which images in your delivery are which. We do not generate a model who was never there and present her as a photograph.

Who owns the content?

You own the right to use the delivered files for the usage and period you selected. LUTU keeps the underlying files and the right to show the work in our own portfolio unless you ask us not to. Models have their own usage limits. The content licence summary sets out exactly what each option allows.

How do I pay, and in what currency?

US dollars. 50% deposit to book your date, the balance before delivery. No payment is taken on this website today — we invoice you directly.

Do Saudi taxes apply?

Our prices exclude any withholding tax or VAT that applies on your side. LUTU is a South African company invoicing from South Africa; how that is treated in your jurisdiction depends on your own tax position, so check with your accountant.
