Content licence summary

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This summary explains, in plain words, what you may do with the content we deliver. It forms part of our terms of service.

Who owns what

LUTU owns the photographs, video and the underlying raw files. You receive a licence to use the delivered files — the finished images and videos — for the usage and period you selected and paid for. You do not acquire the copyright unless we agree that separately in writing.

What each usage option allows

  • Organic social only. Posting on your own brand accounts — Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and similar. No paid promotion of those posts.
  • Paid ads. Everything above, plus running the same content as paid advertising on any platform.
  • Website and marketplaces. Everything above, plus your own website and your listings on Salla, Zid, Noon and comparable marketplaces.

Period

Your licence runs for the period you chose — six months, one year, or unlimited — starting from delivery. When a fixed period ends you should stop using the files in new placements. You do not have to delete posts that are already live unless a model's own agreement requires it.

What you may not do

  • Resell, sublicense or give the files to another brand or seller.
  • Use a model's image in a way that implies she personally endorses or recommends a product.
  • Use the content for anything political, or in a context that is defamatory or offensive.
  • Alter an image so that a product looks materially different from the real product.

Models

Every model signs a release covering the usage you have bought. Some models limit particular territories or categories; if that affects your shoot we tell you before the shoot day. Extending usage later is possible but may need the model's agreement and a further fee.

Our own portfolio

We may show the work in LUTU's portfolio and social channels. If your launch is confidential, tell us and we will hold the work back until you release it, or indefinitely if you prefer.
