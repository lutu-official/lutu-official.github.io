Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

How LUTU orders are made and delivered

Every garment is made to order. Nothing is held in stock. Your garment is manufactured after your order is confirmed and your measurements are received, then shipped to South Africa and delivered to you.

Timing

Because each garment is made individually and shipped internationally, we quote a production and delivery window, not a fixed date. Your window is confirmed to you in writing when your order is placed, before you pay your deposit.

We will tell you when your garment enters production, when it ships, and when it clears customs.

If your order is delayed

If we expect to miss your confirmed delivery window, we will tell you as soon as we know, with a revised window. If the revised window does not work for you, you may cancel for a full refund of everything you have paid, including your deposit, whatever stage production has reached.

We do not keep a deposit on a garment we have failed to deliver on time.

Where we deliver

South Africa. Delivery is to a physical street address; we cannot deliver to a post box. Someone must be available to sign.

For delivery outside South Africa, contact us before ordering.

Delivery cost

Delivery within South Africa is quoted with your order and confirmed in writing before you pay. Import duties and taxes on the consignment are our responsibility, not yours — you will not receive a customs bill for a LUTU garment delivered within South Africa.

Risk and insurance

Your garment remains at our risk until it is delivered to you. Shipments are insured in transit.

If something arrives damaged

Tell us within 7 days of delivery, with photographs. See our Refund Policy.
