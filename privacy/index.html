Who processes your information

Luca Bold, trading as LUTU (sole proprietor), Cape Town, South Africa. Contact: [CONTACT_EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Waitlist and enquiries: your name, WhatsApp number, email address, and anything you choose to tell us in your message.
  • Orders: your delivery address, body measurements, the name to be embroidered on your garment, and your order and payment history.
  • Payments: we do not collect or store your card details. Card payments are processed by Payfast (Network International South Africa). We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded and the last digits of the card, nothing more.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, to make and deliver your garment, to personalise it correctly, to keep you informed about your order, and to meet our record-keeping obligations.

Who we share it with

  • Our manufacturer, for the measurements and personalisation details needed to make your garment. Our manufacturer is located in China.
  • Our freight forwarder and customs clearing agent, for the details needed to ship and clear your garment.
  • Our payment processor, for the details needed to process your payment.

Your information is transferred outside South Africa in the course of manufacturing. We share only what is necessary to produce and deliver your order.

We do not sell your information. We do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and waitlist details: until you ask us to delete them, or three years after our last contact, whichever comes first. Order records: five years, as required for tax and record-keeping.

Your rights under POPIA

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at https://inforegulator.org.za.
