Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who is responsible

LUTU Creative (Pty) Ltd, a South African company based in Cape Town, registration number 2026/936736/07, is the responsible party for the information described here. Contact us at hello@lutu.co.za.

This policy is written to meet South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and, for clients in Saudi Arabia, the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, brand, country, email address, what you sell, when you need content, your Instagram link, and anything you write in the message field or attach.
  • Quote builder: the options you select and the resulting estimate. These are sent with your enquiry so we can price it.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us, your phone number and the content of your messages.
  • Shipping and production: once a shoot is booked, the delivery details needed to return your products.

We do not collect card or banking details through this website. No payment is taken here.

Why we collect it

To answer your enquiry, prepare and confirm your quote, run your shoot, deliver your content, return your products, and keep the records a company is required to keep.

Where it is stored

We do not use a web form. Enquiries reach us by WhatsApp or by email. WhatsApp messages are held by WhatsApp; email is held by Google Workspace. Production files are held in our own storage. Some of these providers store data outside South Africa and outside Saudi Arabia.

Who we share it with

Only the people needed to do the work: the crew on your shoot, the courier returning your products, and the form and email providers above. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become bookings: until you ask us to delete them, or three years after our last contact, whichever is sooner. Booked projects: five years, for tax and record-keeping.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Email hello@lutu.co.za and we will respond within a reasonable time.

In South Africa you may complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za. In Saudi Arabia you may complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies. If we add analytics we will use a privacy-friendly provider that does not use cookies or track you across sites, and this section will be updated.
