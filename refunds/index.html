Refund Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

LUTU garments are made to order to your measurements and carry your name. That affects what can be refunded and when. This policy sets out exactly where the line falls.

Before production begins

You may cancel your order and receive a full refund of your deposit at any time before you give written approval for personalisation. No reason is required and no fee is charged.

After you approve personalisation

Once you have given written sign-off and personalisation work has begun, the garment is made uniquely for you and cannot be resold. From that point we retain the deposit to cover materials and work already committed.

If you cancel after this point, contact us. Where work has not in fact started, or where only part of the committed cost has been incurred, we will refund the difference. We do not retain more than the cost we have actually committed.

If the garment is faulty or not as described

Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act apply in full. If your garment is defective, not of the quality described, or does not match the specification you approved, you may return it within six months of delivery for repair, replacement or refund. We pay return shipping in these cases.

This does not cover fit issues arising from inaccurate measurements supplied to us, or normal wear.

If we cannot deliver

If we are unable to produce or deliver your garment for any reason, you receive a full refund of everything you have paid, including your deposit, regardless of what stage production has reached.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are made to the original payment method within 10 business days of us agreeing the refund.
